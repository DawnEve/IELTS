10 Psychology facts about People

1. People who feel shy or awkward are usually very loyal in relationships.

2. Men tend to eat more when they are having a meal with women rather than with other men.

3. Hearing one negative comment can hurt up to five happy memories.

4. If you think a famous person loves you, it might mean you have a mental health issue.

5. People who aren't considered attractive often think they look better than they do, while attractive people tend to think they aren't as good-looking.

6. Those who get sarcasm are often good at understanding what others are thinking.

7. People who feel guilty usually understand how others feel better.

8. If someone talks quickly but doesn't say much, they might be good at keeping secrets.

9. Copying someone else's behavior in small ways can make you more likable. This is known as the chameleon effect.

10. Kids with strict parents may become better at lying.
